    Method & caveats
    • Data: data/v_series_daily_21d.csv (daily rows, one per series and local day) and data/v_series_summary_21d.csv (one row per series: the card headlines). Both are dumps of the SQLite views of the same names; each series covers its own last 21 local days.
    • Day windows: instantaneous fields use local [00:00, 24:00); precipitation uses hours ending in (00:00, 24:00]; nights run [12:00 d, 12:00 d+1) with the sun below the horizon.
    • Altitudes: temperature is lapse-rate adjusted to the series altitude; precipitation, its rain/snow split and cloud cover come unchanged from the model grid cell, so those cards show the grid-cell altitude (point_elevation_m).
    • Headlines are ratios of 21-day totals; the freezing-level and night-cloud means are hour-weighted (see the view definition).
    • Only type = 'forecast' series are charted; other rows are ignored and counted below the altitude buttons.
    • Basemap: data/basemap.json: terrain from the Open-Meteo elevation API (0.05° grid, 80 m steps), borders from Natural Earth 1:10m. Sites outside its extent are listed but not drawn.
